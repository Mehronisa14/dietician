## Brand & Style

This design system blends evidence-based medical authority with empathetic holistic hospitality. Crafted for a specialized clinical dietician practice, the aesthetic rejects the cold sterility of institutional healthcare in favor of an organic, warm editorial clinical style.

- **Brand Personality**: Scholarly, reassuring, deeply attentive, and scientifically grounded.
- **Target Audience**: Individuals managing chronic metabolic conditions, gut health complexities, hormone dysregulation, and proactive preventative wellness seekers requiring structured, non-judgmental guidance.
- **Emotional Response**: Grounded confidence, relief, clarity, and genuine warmth.
- **Visual Design Archetype**: Warm Editorial Healthcare. Combines literary serif typography, expansive breathing room, tactile organic surfaces, and precise clinical structure.

## Colors

The palette grounds itself in restorative botanical greens and deep maritime navy, lifted by warm energizing coral for decisive transactional cues.

- **Primary (`#3B6E52`)**: A restorative, botanical sage green evoking vitality, gut health, and mindful nutrition. Applied to active states, key icons, badges, and focal interactive accents.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E07A5F`)**: A warm terracotta coral reserved strictly for conversion focal points: primary "Book Appointment" CTAs, urgency indicators, and vibrant highlight badges.
- **Tertiary (`#153243`)**: A deep, authoritative clinical teal-slate utilized for prominent editorial headlines, high-trust stat counters, and formal boundary elements.
- **Neutral Text (`#2D3748`)**: A tempered slate charcoal ensuring WCAG AAA legibility without the harshness of pure black.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds**: The base canvas employs a soft parchment off-white (`#FAF9F6`), secondary modules sit on an airy sage mist (`#F4F7F4`), while cards and inputs use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to establish crisp contrast layers.
- **Dividers & Outlines**: Soft sage tint (`#E2EBE4`) for delicate, non-invasive structural definition.

## Typography

The typographic pairing juxtaposes the literary, dignified gravitas of **Newsreader** (headings) with the clear, humanist rhythm of **Plus Jakarta Sans** (body and UI labels).

- **Newsreader**: Conveys the medical authority of clinical journals while maintaining a warm bedside manner. Used exclusively for storytelling, hero statements, module titles, and patient testimonial excerpts. Italic variations are encouraged for scientific names, gentle emphasis, or reflective subheadings.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Provides functional, highly legible ergonomics across dense clinical plans, appointment schedules, nutrient breakdowns, and form inputs.
- **Formatting Guidelines**: Never display body copy in uppercase. Clinical labels, dietary tags, and overlines should use `label-sm` with slight uppercase tracking (0.05em) for neat visual anchoring.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid provides clinical structure, while expansive outer vertical pacing creates a relaxed, unhurried reading cadence.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (<768px), Tablet (768px–1024px), Desktop (>1024px).
- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max container width of 1240px centered, flanked by dynamic canvas margins (`margin: 3rem`) and consistent gutters (`gutter: 1.5rem`). Content flows cleanly across 4-column clinical pillars, 6-column split views, and 8-column editorial reading tracks.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Gutters adjust to `1.25rem`; cards collapse from 3-up to 2-up configurations with preserved vertical flow.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Horizontal margins contract to `1.25rem` (`margin-mobile`), ensuring maximal surface area for reading medical protocols and completing booking steps.
- **Rhythm**: Sections maintain generous breathing space (`space-2xl` to `5rem` top/bottom padding) to avoid sensory overload common in traditional medical directories.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows, instead utilizing warm atmospheric depth and tonal surface layering to express hierarchy.

- **Tonal Layers**: The foundational canvas rests on `#FAF9F6`. Elevated content blocks use pure white `#FFFFFF` paired with structural borders in `#E2EBE4`. Informative callout panels rest on `#F4F7F4`.
- **Atmospheric Shadows**:
  - *Resting Cards*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(21, 50, 67, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(21, 50, 67, 0.02)`. A soft tint derived from the tertiary clinical teal ensures natural integration.
  - *Interactive Hover & Sticky Elements*: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(21, 50, 67, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(21, 50, 67, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Triggers (WhatsApp CTA, Sticky Nav)*: `0 8px 24px rgba(33, 53, 41, 0.12)`, blending botanical green with slate for an organic floating effect.
- **Backdrop Filters**: The sticky navigation header implements a frosted warm glass effect: `rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle `1px` border of `#E2EBE4`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, biological radius strategy (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the natural curves of organic vitality while maintaining clinical composure.

- **Base Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg` / 16px to `rounded-xl` / 24px)**: Service cards, nutrition plans, and booking containers employ a gentle 16px–24px corner curve to eliminate aggressive sharp medical corners.
- **Input Fields & Interactive Containers**: Standardized at `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for neat thumb ergonomics and tidy structure.
- **Buttons, Dietary Badges & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) or `0.75rem` (12px), creating approachable, tactile touchpoints.
- **Floating Contact Hubs**: WhatsApp action triggers utilize pure circular profiles (56px × 56px, `rounded-full`) for immediate distinction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Book Appointment")**: Background `#E07A5F`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-md`, fully pill-shaped with `0.875rem` vertical and `1.75rem` horizontal padding. Hover elevates with `translateY(-1px)` and a deepened coral tint (`#D66A4E`).
- **Secondary Clinical Action**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #3B6E52`, text `#3B6E52`. Hover shifts surface to `rgba(59, 110, 82, 0.08)`.
- **Subtle Ghost Button**: Text `#153243`, padding `0.5rem 1rem`, text decoration none, subtle underline indicator on focus.

### Service & Condition Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2EBE4`, radius `1.25rem` (20px), padding `1.75rem`.
- Features an icon badge at the top left housed in a 44px circular container (`#F4F7F4` with a `#3B6E52` botanical glyph).
- Contains a clinical title in `headline-sm` (`#153243`), succinct scope of treatment in `body-sm`, and an inline text link ("Explore Protocol →").

### Form Fields & Interactive Booking
- Inputs utilize an off-white fill (`#FAF9F6`), transitioning to `#FFFFFF` on active focus.
- Border is `1px solid #E2EBE4`; on focus, changes to `1.5px solid #3B6E52` accompanied by a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 110, 82, 0.15)`.
- Labels are rendered in `label-sm` (`#153243`), placed cleanly above the input with a `0.375rem` gap.

### Care Timeline (Step-by-Step Patient Journey)
- Vertical rail using a soft `2px` rule of `#E2EBE4`.
- Milestones marked by numbered or checked node rings (32px diameter, `#3B6E52` fill with white label or `#F4F7F4` unfilled for future stages).
- Adjacent content blocks clarify the assessment, diagnostic, lab analysis, and meal strategy steps.

### Accordions (FAQ & Evidence Base)
- Full width with divider borders in `#E2EBE4`.
- Header toggle uses `headline-sm` or `label-lg` in `#153243`, expanding smoothly with a botanical green rotating indicator icon (`+` to `−`).
- Expanded answer container set in `body-md` (`#2D3748`) with `padding-bottom: 1.5rem`.

### Trust Badges & Floating WhatsApp Action
- **Trust Badges**: Compact pill chips combining recognized clinical accreditation marks (e.g., Registered Dietitian, Master of Science) with a `#F4F7F4` surface, `#3B6E52` typography, and soft sage border.
- **WhatsApp Floating Button**: Fixed 24px from bottom right, 56px circle, vibrant WhatsApp emerald (`#25D366`) or brand sage (`#3B6E52`), housing a crisp white message glyph and accompanied by a responsive tooltip: *"Chat with clinical care team"*.
