Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our clinical consultations, lab requirements, and care protocols.

How long does an initial consultation take? expand_more
The initial clinical consultation lasts 60 minutes. This provides dedicated time to thoroughly explore your full medical history, past diagnoses, lab reports, current pharmacological treatments, dietary preferences, and sleep/stress patterns.
What lab reports or medical records should I bring? expand_more
Please share any laboratory investigations conducted within the past 6 to 12 months. Most helpful panels include Complete Blood Count (CBC), Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP), HbA1c, Fasting Insulin, Lipid Profile (Total, LDL, HDL, Triglycerides), Thyroid Panel (TSH, Free T3/T4), and renal parameters (eGFR, Creatinine). If you don't have recent tests, we can advise specific panels during our first visit.
How do online / telehealth consultations work? expand_more
Telehealth sessions occur through a secure, encrypted HIPAA-compliant video link provided upon booking confirmation. You can participate comfortably from your computer or smartphone. We screen-share biomarker charts, meal breakdowns, and interactive guide sheets identically to an in-office experience.
How many follow-up sessions will I typically need? expand_more
For focused goals, clients often require 3 to 4 sessions spaced across 8 to 12 weeks. Patients managing multi-factorial clinical conditions (such as advanced kidney disease, severe insulin resistance, or chronic GI inflammation) benefit most from our comprehensive 12-week continuity programs.
Are consultations covered by health insurance? expand_more
As a licensed Registered Dietitian (RD), our clinical care receipts contain official diagnostic billing and procedure codes (CPT 97802 & 97803 for Medical Nutrition Therapy). Many private insurance policies and HSA/FSA plans reimburse for these preventive clinical services. We provide complete itemized superbills upon request.

Have an urgent medical nutrition inquiry?

Our clinical intake nurse is available via WhatsApp for immediate appointment scheduling, physician referrals, and program advice.
